Foundational Research
User & Non-User Perceptions of AI
Explored how users and non-users perceive generative AI tools to inform TikTok's AI communications and product marketing strategy.
Background

As TikTok continues to roll out AI-powered features across the platform, understanding how users perceive generative AI tools is critical. These perceptions shape user receptivity and directly inform the messaging strategy when introducing and discussing AI tools. This foundational qualitative study explored perceptions of AI in general and TikTok's use of AI specifically, generating early learnings to guide communications, media outreach, and product marketing strategies.

Research Objectives
  • Understand users' awareness, attitudes, and mental models of AI
  • Explore perceptions of TikTok's use of AI — including perceived benefits, concerns, and impact on platform experience
Methodology

1:1 semi-structured interviews with both non-users and users across varying levels of AI usage and awareness.

Semi-Structured Interviews Qualitative Users & Non-Users
Key Findings
Users are aware that AI tools are rapidly expanding on social media platforms.
Users view AI as a helpful tool that can aid with planning, synthesizing, and learning new things.
Trust in AI hinges on accuracy and the ability to help complete intended tasks.
Users are concerned that over-relying on AI will impact critical thinking skills and interrupt online experiences.
The dominant mental model: AI is a helpful coworker that can make mistakes.
TikTok users expect the platform to roll out AI features aligned with their in-app behaviors.
Recommendations
01
Position TikTok's AI tools as assistants that handle the "heavy lifting" of searching for information, helping users locate content at a quicker rate.
02
Emphasize utility over underlying technology. Messaging should prioritize simple, jargon-free language that focuses on how the tool assists the user.
03
Promote AI tools as experience enhancers rather than interrupters — tools that work with how users already like to use the app.
